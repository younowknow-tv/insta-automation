# Static card backlog

Cards are built by `cards.py` from `content/cards/<id>.json`; finished PNGs go to
`output/static/` (local only). Two layouts, alternated — never blended:
`ink` (flat colour, text only) and `photo` (full-bleed archival image, dimmed).

Cadence (Gaurav, 2026-10-09): **weekdays = cards**, ink most days and a photo
carousel on some; **Friday and weekend = reels**.

Card topics do **not** have to come from reel research. Standalone facts count,
and we pick them together the way we pick reel topics.

Status: `idea` → `verified` → `built` → `posted`
